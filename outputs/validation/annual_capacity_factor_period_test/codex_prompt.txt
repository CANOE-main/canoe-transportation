Why this feature is needed

An annual utilization factor describes how much service a unit of available capacity
provides each year. Vintage identifies a cohort; period identifies when it operates.
Demand/consumer technologies need both: an individual vehicle cohort can be driven
less as it ages, and charging infrastructure can have changing utilization as transport
electrifies. A vintage-only factor freezes each cohort's utilization through time;
a period-only factor cannot distinguish cohorts operating in the same period. Either
limitation can bias the capacity required to meet future demand. This feature must
allow both dimensions without changing existing models' meaning.

The supplied transport test input derives road utilization from CEUD annual
activity/stock and configured capacity_to_activity, then scales it with normalized ATB
age-mileage profiles. It supplies a realistic example of a cohort's changing annual
service output. These factors are dimensionless; they are not efficiency,
retirement/survival, or hourly availability. Charger period variation is another use
case; do not reinterpret today's reviewed charger policies as a newly approved
calendar-period trajectory.
