# CANOE-Transportation Assumptions

This review record preserves source limitations and their modeling choices handling them. Tables are sorted by technology scope. Tags distinguish exceptions `#edge-case`, new backend behavior `#legacy-changes` and choices shared by parameters `#multi-parameter`. Review markers do not establish acceptance. Full source identities and citations are in the [source registry](../config/sources.yaml). Exhaustive breakdown of assumptions found in [rules](../config/parameters/rules.yaml) and selection of modeling choices in [scenario configuration](../config/scenarios/default.yaml).

LDVs comprise cars and passenger/freight light trucks. Medium/heavy trucks exclude buses unless named; the source-family term MHDV can include buses. Road vehicles include buses and motorcycles. Non-road modes are air, rail and marine; off-road equipment is a separate modeled category. BEVs are battery EVs; PHEVs are plug-in hybrids; PEVs include both. BCT combines British Columbia and the territories.

CS/CD mean charge-sustaining/charge-depleting operation; UF is the distance utility factor, HHV higher heating value, C2A capacity-to-activity, and M&R maintenance and repair. GVWR is a vehicle's rated fully loaded weight. *CER CEF* denotes Canada's Energy Future. Source years, model vintages and monetary dollar years are distinct.
